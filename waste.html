Under the Hood

From pixels to proper disposal.

A four-step pipeline runs every time you scan an image — from raw pixels to actionable disposal guidance in under a second.

01 — CAPTURE

Image ingestion

Upload a photo or use your camera. The image is resized and normalised for the detection model without leaving your browser.

02 — DETECT

Object localisation

A YOLO-based backbone scans the frame, drawing bounding boxes around each distinct waste item it finds.

03 — CLASSIFY

Category mapping

Each detected region is passed through a ResNet classifier trained on 200+ waste categories with recycling ground-truth labels.

04 — GUIDE

Disposal guidance

Results are mapped to local waste regulations and displayed with confidence scores and exact bin instructions.
